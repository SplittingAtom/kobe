# Getting started

Kobe is where your team talks to AI agents. Each agent works in its own sandbox: it can read and
write files, run code and use the tools your team has connected, and it asks before it does
anything that needs approval.

## Sign in

Open Kobe in your browser and sign in with your email and password or a passkey. If you belong to
more than one team, pick the team at the top of the left rail.

## Start a conversation

Type what you need in the composer at the bottom of the screen and press Enter. The agent answers
in the thread; you can follow up, attach files or stop it at any time.

New to the words Kobe uses? See the [Glossary](glossary.md#thread).
