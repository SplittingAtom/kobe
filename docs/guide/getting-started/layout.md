Kobe's screen has four parts: a bar along the top, the rail on the left, the conversation in the
middle and the artifact column on the right. Each column is a floating card you can show or hide.
This page tells you what lives where.

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **The site name or logo.**
- **Thread search**: type to find a conversation by its title or content.
- **My area**: **My memory**, **My schedules**, **Agents** and **Skills**.
- **Site console**, shown only to site admins. See [Site admin overview](../admin/site-admin.md).
- **Notifications.**
- **Dark theme**: switches between light and dark. See [Appearance](../appearance.md).
- **Profile**: **Projects**, **My connectors**, **My approvals**, **My shared cases** and
  **Sign out**.

## The rail

The rail is the left column. Its top line holds, in order:

- the **Workspace** switcher (the workspace's name; a menu when you belong to several);
- **New conversation**;
- **Threads panel**, which opens your conversations over the screen (see
  [The Threads panel](../chat/threads-panel.md));
- **Files panel**, which shows or hides your files in the rail;
- **Team console**, shown only to team admins (see [Team admin overview](../admin/team-admin.md));
- **Collapse the rail** (Ctrl+B or Cmd+B toggles it).

Below the top line are two tabs. **Files** shows the workspace tree: your projects and **My
files**. **Apps** appears only when a site admin has placed an app in the rail for you; it runs one
picker app you choose from its list. See [Connectors and apps](../connectors-and-apps.md).

## The conversation column

The middle column is the conversation on screen. At the top are its title (for a conversation in
a project, "Project / name"; click the name to rename it) and the **Conversation tree** button.
The thread's other actions are in its row menu in the Threads panel, in the command palette and as
slash commands. If the
thread was forked from another one, a lineage strip above it links back to the original. The
message box sits at the bottom with its toolbar: the **+** menu, the agent, the model and the
thinking level. Under it a context gauge shows how full the model's context is; click it to see
what the model was sent. See [Writing messages](../chat/composer.md).

## The artifact column

The right column holds tabs for things you open: files from the Files panel, artifacts the agent
made, app cards and notebooks. It keeps up to 20 tabs. Show or hide it with **Show or hide the
artifact column** or Ctrl+Shift+A (Cmd+Shift+A). Files open in the editor there; see
[Editing files](../files/file-editor.md).

## On a phone

Below 768 pixels wide the rail becomes a drawer. The bar shows a button with the workspace's name
(**Show the workspace**) that opens it, and a **Menu** button for the rest of the bar. Close the
drawer with **Close the workspace**. Wide-only features, such as the lines between threads and
files in the Threads panel, are not shown on narrow screens; their lists still work.

## Getting around by keyboard

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K) to search actions, threads and files, or **?** to see every shortcut. See
[Keyboard shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).
